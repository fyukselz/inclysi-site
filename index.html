Most of the queue is never read.

A mid-size SIEM raises thousands of incidents a day. Analysts clear what they can, suppress what they can’t, and the rest expires unread. The threats that matter are in there, averaged out by everything that doesn’t.

Another dashboard doesn’t change that. Reading every incident, deciding on each one and writing down why, does.
